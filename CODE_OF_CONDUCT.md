# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the HostMK Security Framework community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Security Research and Responsible Disclosure

HostMK Security Framework encourages responsible security research.

Researchers are expected to:

- Report vulnerabilities responsibly.
- Avoid accessing data that does not belong to them.
- Avoid disrupting production systems.
- Follow coordinated disclosure practices.

Security issues should be reported through the process described in SECURITY.md.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing community standards.

Maintainers may:

- Remove inappropriate content.
- Edit or reject harmful contributions.
- Temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- GitHub Discussions
- Pull Requests
- Issues
- Documentation
- Community forums
- Official communication channels

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

security@hostmk.com

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction

Community Impact:
Use of inappropriate language or minor unprofessional behavior.

Consequence:
Private warning and request for corrective action.

### 2. Warning

Community Impact:
Repeated inappropriate behavior.

Consequence:
Formal warning with temporary restrictions.

### 3. Temporary Ban

Community Impact:
Serious violation of community standards.

Consequence:
Temporary suspension from participation.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Community Impact:
Sustained harassment, discrimination, threats, or malicious conduct.

Consequence:
Permanent removal from project spaces.
